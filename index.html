How it works

A dietary supplement can say it supports how the body works. It can’t say it treats, cures or prevents a disease. FDA defines where that line sits, and Claims Lab checks your wording against it.

Paste your copy

Label text, ad copy, product pages. Each sentence is checked the moment you type.

Rules, not guesses

48 rules mapped to FDA’s ten disease-claim criteria, tested against the examples in FDA’s own compliance guide.

Clear next steps

Every finding links to its rule, explains why, and suggests structure/function wording where one exists.

What it can’t check

No flags doesn’t mean cleared. These are automated pattern checks, not legal or regulatory advice.

    Claims Lab is an open-source tool from Zee Palm Labs. Findings map wording to 21 CFR 101.93 and 21 U.S.C. 343(r)(6), using the criteria and examples in FDA’s Small Entity Compliance Guide. Suggested wording is a template, not a regulatory determination. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
