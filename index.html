HOW IT WORKS

Rules, not guesses

A supplement can say it supports how the body works. It can’t say it treats, cures or prevents a disease. FDA defines where that line sits — Claims Lab checks your wording against it.

01

Paste your copy

Label text, ad copy or product pages. Every sentence is checked the moment you type.

02

Checked against FDA’s criteria

48 rules mapped to FDA’s ten disease-claim criteria and tested against the examples in its guidance.

03

Fix it with confidence

Each finding cites its rule, explains why, and suggests structure/function wording where one exists.

What it can’t check

No flags doesn’t mean cleared. These are automated checks, not legal or regulatory advice.

    Claims Lab is a free, open-source tool from Zee Palm Labs. Findings map wording to 21 CFR 101.93 and 21 U.S.C. 343(r)(6) using the criteria and examples in FDA’s Small Entity Compliance Guide. Suggested wording is a template, not a regulatory determination. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
