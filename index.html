What this can’t check

    Automated pattern checks, not legal or regulatory advice. Each finding maps wording to a paragraph of 21 CFR 101.93 or 21 U.S.C. 343(r)(6), using the criteria and examples in FDA’s Small Entity Compliance Guide. A claim with no flags has not been cleared — it simply didn’t match these rules. Suggested rewrites are templates, not regulatory determinations. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
